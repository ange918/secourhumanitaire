About Fode

A small kitchen with big flavor

Fode started with one pan and one sauce. Today our chefs cook pasta, pizza and dumplings the same way: fresh ingredients, patient cooking and a lot of love. No frozen shortcuts, ever.

  • Dough made by hand every morning
  • Sauces simmered for six hours
  • Recyclable, heat-keeping packaging
